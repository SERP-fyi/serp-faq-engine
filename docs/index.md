## General

### 1. What is SERP.fyi?

An advanced search intelligence platform that combines real-time Google search tracking with AI visibility audits.

### 2. Who is SERP.fyi built for?

SEO professionals, digital marketing agencies, brand managers, and CMOs tracking modern search environments.

### 3. How is SERP.fyi different from traditional SEO tools?

Traditional tools only track keyword rankings, whereas SERP.fyi tracks how both human search engines and AI systems understand your brand.

### 15. Who created SERP.fyi?

It is an advanced search observatory tool developed as part of the BHMarketer ecosystem.

### 30. Does SERP.fyi require any software installation?

No, it is a cloud-based web platform accessible right from your browser.

## AI Visibility and Citations

### 4. What is an AI visibility audit?

An analysis measuring how often, accurately, and positively your brand is cited and recommended across AI search engines.

### 5. Which AI search platforms does SERP.fyi monitor?

Major generative search platforms and LLMs, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

### 16. What is entity recognition tracking?

It measures whether AI systems recognize your brand as a legitimate, established entity within their knowledge graphs.

### 17. Why are AI citations important for my brand?

Modern users often get answers directly from AI summaries without visiting traditional links, making AI citations vital for exposure.

### 23. Can SERP.fyi help optimize content for Google AI Overviews?

Yes, by showing how your content performs against the specific criteria required to win AI summary citations.

### 28. What does "LLM-Ready Signal Mapping" mean?

It identifies the specific structural and trust signals determining whether an AI model recommends your brand.

### 29. How do I check if my brand is mentioned by ChatGPT or Perplexity?

The platform's AI visibility scanner queries these generative models to track direct brand mentions and context.

### 33. What should I do if my AI visibility score is low?

You can improve your entity footprint by building structured data, digital PR, and authoritative web mentions that LLMs frequently scrape.

### 37. Does SERP.fyi track voice or conversational search trends?

Yes, by monitoring AI-driven answer engines, it covers the core components of conversational search discovery.

## SERP Features and Signals

### 18. How does SERP.fyi handle zero-click searches?

It tracks how your brand appears inside SERP features and AI answer blocks where traditional clicks are intercepted.

### 21. What are SERP features?

### 24. What is signal mapping?

The process of mapping out underlying signals, such as schema, authority, and structure, that influence your search placement.
